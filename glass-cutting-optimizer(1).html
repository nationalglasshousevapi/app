Engine: for each new sheet, every checked stock size — in both orientations — is tried and the one with best yield (used area minus a scrap penalty) is chosen. After the main row-pack, remaining pieces are re-tried against every leftover strip before it's scored as remnant/scrap. Heuristic, not a guaranteed global optimum.
